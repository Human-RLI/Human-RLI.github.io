Human-Centered Robot Learning
and Interaction

HumanRLI 2026
Toward Reliable and Scalable Autonomy in Human Environments
November 12, 2026Austin, Texas, USAIn-person

Robot learning has made rapid progress on dexterous manipulation, locomotion, navigation, and increasingly general vision-language-action models. Yet real-world deployment means operating in human environments and around humans — homes, hospitals, offices, warehouses, public spaces — where people are not only end-users but also collaborators, supervisors, demonstrators, and bystanders. This workshop asks how we translate lab-scale demonstrations into systems reliable enough to be trusted and scalable enough to deploy across diverse users, tasks, and settings.

Call for Papers — Representative Topics

1 Learning from Human Data

Scalable interaction data · demonstrations, corrections, preferences · continual learning from feedback · privacy, consent, effort

2 Human-Aware Foundation Models

Grounding VLA models in contact and social norms · human attention, intent, mental states · personalization and preference alignment

3 Reliability, Safety, Evaluation

Benchmarks beyond task success · robustness to clutter, interruptions, novice users · failure detection, recovery, graceful handover

4 Long-Horizon Interaction

Turn-taking, intent inference, interaction repair · multi-session memory · field studies in homes, hospitals, warehouses
